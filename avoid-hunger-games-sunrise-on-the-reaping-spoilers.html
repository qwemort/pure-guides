The book is already out

Most films get spoiled after the premiere. This one has been spoilable since March 2025: book summaries, reaction videos and fan edits sit under every trailer, and book readers quote the ending in comments without warning.

Film details then come from premieres and press screenings a few days before November 20, and "book vs movie" breakdowns appear on opening night. Do not mute plain "Hunger Games": it also hides the older films and harmless news.

Where to paste them

XNotifications tab, gear icon, Muted, Muted words. Pick "Forever".
Threads, InstagramThreads: Profile, Menu, Content preferences, Hidden words, New filter. Instagram keeps its own list in Content preferences, Specific words and phrases (suggested posts only).
TikTokProfile, Menu, Settings and privacy, Content preferences, Filter keywords. Works in the For You and Following feeds, up to 100 words.
YouTube"Not interested" and "Don't recommend channel". Comments and Shorts stay unfiltered.
RedditMute communities from their menu. Titles in r/popular still get through.
DiscordMute busy servers and channels for the first weeks.

Questions

When does Sunrise on the Reaping come out?

It is in cinemas from November 20, 2026. Some countries get it a few days later.

Is it based on a book?

Yes, on the 2025 novel by Suzanne Collins, so the main story is widely discussed online.

Which words should I mute?

The film title with words like ending, spoilers, who dies and book vs movie. Muting only "Hunger Games" also hides the older films.

Can I mute words on YouTube?

No. YouTube has no keyword mute. In a desktop browser an extension can blur matching videos and comments.
