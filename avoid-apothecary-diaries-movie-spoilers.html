Japan sees it first

With a worldwide release everyone sees a film in the same week. Here Japan gets it on December 11 and no international date has been announced, so for a while the film is discussed on Japanese X, translated by fan accounts and summarised in YouTube videos. The longer the wait, the more of it reaches feeds outside Japan.

The story is original, written by the author Natsu Hyuga, not adapted from the novels or manga. Readers cannot warn you and you cannot guess the names from the books. Season 3 is airing at the same time, so mute film words instead of the series title if you still want the episodes.

Where to paste them

XNotifications tab, gear icon, Muted, Muted words. Pick "Forever".
Threads, InstagramThreads: Profile, Menu, Content preferences, Hidden words, New filter. Instagram keeps its own list in Content preferences, Specific words and phrases (suggested posts only).
TikTokProfile, Menu, Settings and privacy, Content preferences, Filter keywords. Works in the For You and Following feeds, up to 100 words.
YouTube"Not interested" and "Don't recommend channel". Comments and Shorts stay unfiltered.
RedditMute communities from their menu. Titles in r/popular still get through.
DiscordMute busy servers and channels for the first weeks.

Questions

When does The Apothecary Diaries movie come out?

It opens in Japanese cinemas on December 11, 2026. An international date has not been announced yet.

Is the story from the novels?

No. It is a new story by the author Natsu Hyuga, so readers of the novels and manga do not know it either.

Which words should I mute?

The film title in English, romaji and Japanese, with words like ending, spoilers and who dies. Japanese spoilers come first.

Can I mute words on YouTube?

No. YouTube has no keyword mute. In a desktop browser an extension can do it.
