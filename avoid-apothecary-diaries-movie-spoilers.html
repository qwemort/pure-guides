Updated 17 November 2026

The Apothecary Diaries film (The Late Consort's Hidden Treasure) opens in Japanese cinemas on December 11, 2026. No international date has been announced yet (source). Its story is new, written by the author Natsu Hyuga, so book and manga readers do not know it either. Everyone outside Japan will be waiting, possibly for weeks, while viewers in Japan talk about it.

Short version
  1. Spoilers will come in Japanese first. Mute the Japanese title too.
  2. Mute the words below before December 11.
  3. Be careful with fan art and clips from Japan: they spread on X with no warning.
  4. Season 3 is airing at the same time, so mute "movie" words without hiding the series.
  5. Plan for the whole gap until the film reaches your country.

Why the wait makes it harder

With a worldwide release, everyone sees the film in the same week. Here, Japan gets it first and the rest of the world later. During that gap the film is discussed on Japanese X, translated in fan accounts and summarised in YouTube videos. The longer the wait, the more of it reaches feeds outside Japan.

New story, so no "safe" readers

The film is an original story, not an adaptation of the novels or manga. That means you cannot ask a friend who has read ahead to warn you, and you cannot guess which names to avoid from the books.

YouTube and Reddit have no word mute

YouTube only offers "Not interested" and "Don't recommend channel". Reddit mutes communities, not words. Thumbnails, titles and comments are where most spoilers land, and there is nothing to type a word into.

What no setting can catch

  • Images and video frames. A thumbnail can show the twist with no words at all.
  • Names nobody knows yet. New characters created for the film cannot be listed until it is out.
  • People. Friends and group chats. Tell them early that you have not seen it.

Questions

When does The Apothecary Diaries movie come out?

It opens in Japanese cinemas on December 11, 2026. An international release date has not been announced yet.

Is the movie based on the novels?

No. It is an original story by the series author Natsu Hyuga, so readers do not know what happens either.

Which words should I mute?

The title in English, romaji (Kusuriya no Hitorigoto) and Japanese (薬屋のひとりごと) together with words like movie, ending and spoilers. Japanese posts will appear first.

Will muting the movie hide Season 3 too?

Only if you mute the plain title. Mute the title together with "movie" or 映画 to keep the series in your feed.
