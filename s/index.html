Make your own spoiler link

Spoiler link: share a spoiler without spoiling anyone

X, Threads, Instagram and YouTube comments have no spoiler tag. Paste your spoiler, get a link. People who open it see a blurred text and decide for themselves.

How it works

  • The spoiler is stored inside the link itself, after the # sign. Browsers never send that part to any server, so it is not stored on this site or anywhere else.
  • Link previews on X, Threads or Telegram show only "Spoiler inside", never the text.
  • Anyone with the link can read it, so do not put private things there.
