How to block Mashle spoilers on YouTube, Reddit and X

Updated 2026-11-14

Short answer. YouTube and Reddit have no way to mute words, so Mashle spoilers in thumbnails, titles and comments get through. A browser extension that reads the page and blurs matching videos, posts and comments is what actually works. I build one, Pure Spoiler Blocker: it is free, ships with a ready-made Mashle list and runs locally.

What the Mashle list hides

  • 9 spellings of the title (Latin, Cyrillic, Japanese/Chinese script), By default it hides everything about Mashle (for a running season almost any news can spoil); switch the level to "spoilers only" to keep plain news and trailers.
  • 19 character and place names from the list. Names that belong only to Mashle are recognised on their own; names that are also ordinary words count only on pages that are already about Mashle.
  • Works on YouTube (home, search, Shorts, comments), Reddit (posts and comments) and X.
  • To see a hidden item, click it twice. Nothing is deleted, only covered.

Honest limits: it reads text, so a spoiler that is only inside a thumbnail image, with no words, can slip through. It works in the desktop browser, not in phone apps.

Set it up in a minute

  1. Install Pure Spoiler Blocker from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open it and turn on Mashle in the list of ready-made titles.
  3. Open YouTube or Reddit. Matching cards and comments are blurred right away.

What each site lets you do without an extension

  • X: muted words. Copy-ready list: Mashle mute list.
  • Threads: Settings, Privacy, Hidden words.
  • YouTube: "Not interested" and "Don't recommend channel" on single videos only.
  • Reddit: mute whole communities, not words.

Questions

Can YouTube block Mashle spoilers by itself?

No. YouTube has no word filter. You can tap "Not interested" or "Don't recommend channel" on single videos, but new spoiler thumbnails, Shorts and comments still show up.

How do I hide Mashle spoilers on Reddit?

Reddit lets you mute whole communities, not words, so posts about Mashle in other subreddits and in search still appear. An extension that reads post titles and comments is the only way to filter by words.

Does X (Twitter) have a filter for Mashle spoilers?

Yes, X has muted words. It hides posts that contain them, but not the same videos on YouTube or posts on Reddit.

Is Pure Spoiler Blocker free?

Yes, all of it. The ready-made Mashle list, the other ready-made lists and as many titles of your own as you like work on YouTube, Reddit and X, and after one click on Google search, TikTok and Instagram. There is no paid version and no account.

Does it collect my data?

No. It runs in your browser with no account and no tracking, and its source code is public.
