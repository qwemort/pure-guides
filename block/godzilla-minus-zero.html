How to block Godzilla Minus Zero spoilers on YouTube, Reddit and X

Updated 2026-11-07

Short answer. YouTube and Reddit have no way to mute words, so Godzilla Minus Zero spoilers in thumbnails, titles and comments get through. Questions

Can YouTube block Godzilla Minus Zero spoilers by itself?

No. YouTube has no word filter. You can tap "Not interested" or "Don't recommend channel" on single videos, but new spoiler thumbnails, Shorts and comments still show up.

How do I hide Godzilla Minus Zero spoilers on Reddit?

Reddit lets you mute whole communities, not words, so posts about Godzilla Minus Zero in other subreddits and in search still appear. An extension that reads post titles and comments is the only way to filter by words.

Does X (Twitter) have a filter for Godzilla Minus Zero spoilers?

Yes, X has muted words. It hides posts that contain them, but not the same videos on YouTube or posts on Reddit.
