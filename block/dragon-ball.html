How to block Dragon Ball spoilers on YouTube, Reddit and X

Updated 2026-11-09

Short answer. YouTube and Reddit have no way to mute words, so Dragon Ball spoilers in thumbnails, titles and comments get through. A browser extension that reads the page and blurs matching videos, posts and comments is what actually works. Questions

Can YouTube block Dragon Ball spoilers by itself?

No. YouTube has no word filter. You can tap "Not interested" or "Don't recommend channel" on single videos, but new spoiler thumbnails, Shorts and comments still show up.

How do I hide Dragon Ball spoilers on Reddit?

Reddit lets you mute whole communities, not words, so posts about Dragon Ball in other subreddits and in search still appear. An extension that reads post titles and comments is the only way to filter by words.

Does X (Twitter) have a filter for Dragon Ball spoilers?

Yes, X has muted words. It hides posts that contain them, but not the same videos on YouTube or posts on Reddit.
