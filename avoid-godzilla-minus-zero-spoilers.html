Out in some countries, not in yours

Japan got it on November 3 and North America on November 6, while other countries get it weeks or months later. So "ending explained" videos, reaction clips and box office news are already everywhere, and the film may not have reached your cinemas yet.

Keep your filters on until you have seen it. Do not mute plain "Godzilla": it also hides Minus One, the MonsterVerse films and news.

Questions

When does Godzilla Minus Zero come out?

It opened in Japan on November 3, 2026 and in North America on November 6, 2026. Other countries follow later.

Is it a sequel to Godzilla Minus One?

Yes, by the same director, Takashi Yamazaki. Spoilers often mention characters from Minus One, so be careful with names.

Which words should I mute?

The film title with words like ending, post credits, who dies and spoilers. Muting only "Godzilla" also hides the other films.

Can I mute words on YouTube?

No. YouTube has no keyword mute. In a desktop browser an extension can blur matching videos and comments.
