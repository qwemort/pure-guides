Short answer. YouTube and Reddit have no way to mute words, so GTA VI spoilers in thumbnails, titles and comments get through. A browser extension that reads the page and blurs matching videos, posts and comments is what actually works. I build one, Pure Spoiler Blocker: it is free, ships with a ready-made GTA VI list and runs locally.

## Try it right here

A made-up YouTube-style feed with no real spoilers. Switch the Shield on and watch the GTA VI spoiler videos blur while trailers and everything else stay (this is the "spoilers only" level).

## What the GTA VI list hides

- 22 spellings of the title (Latin, Cyrillic, Japanese/Chinese, Korean script), By default it hides spoiler talk (ending, leak, twist and the like) and keeps plain news and trailers; switch the level to "everything" to hide all of it.

- 47 character and place names from the list. Names that belong only to GTA VI are recognised on their own; names that are also ordinary words count only on pages that are already about GTA VI.

- Works on YouTube (home, search, Shorts, comments), Reddit (posts and comments) and X.

- To see a hidden item, click it twice. Nothing is deleted, only covered.

Honest limits: it reads text, so a spoiler that is only inside a thumbnail image, with no words, can slip through. It works in the desktop browser, not in phone apps.

## Set it up in a minute

## What each site lets you do without an extension

- X: muted words. Copy-ready list: [GTA VI mute list](../gta-6-mute-list.html).

- Threads: Settings, Privacy, Hidden words.

- YouTube: "Not interested" and "Don't recommend channel" on single videos only.

- Reddit: mute whole communities, not words.

## Questions

### Can YouTube block GTA VI spoilers by itself?

No. YouTube has no word filter. You can tap "Not interested" or "Don't recommend channel" on single videos, but new spoiler thumbnails, Shorts and comments still show up.

### How do I hide GTA VI spoilers on Reddit?

Reddit lets you mute whole communities, not words, so posts about GTA VI in other subreddits and in search still appear. An extension that reads post titles and comments is the only way to filter by words.

### Does X (Twitter) have a filter for GTA VI spoilers?

Yes, X has muted words. It hides posts that contain them, but not the same videos on YouTube or posts on Reddit.

### Is Pure Spoiler Blocker free?

Yes, all of it. The ready-made GTA VI list, the other ready-made lists and as many titles of your own as you like work on YouTube, Reddit and X, and after one click on Google search, TikTok and Instagram. There is no paid version and no account.

### Does it collect my data?

No. It runs in your browser with no account and no tracking, and its source code is public.
