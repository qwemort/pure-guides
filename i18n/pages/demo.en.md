A YouTube-style feed with made-up videos (no real spoilers here). Pick what you have not finished yet and watch the spoiler videos blur, while trailers, guides and everything else stay. Click a blurred card twice to see it.

 Shield 

That is the whole idea. Pure Spoiler Blocker does this on the real YouTube (home, search, Shorts, comments), on Reddit and on X, and after one click on Google search, TikTok and Instagram, for 52 ready-made titles in 13 languages, plus 3 of your own. All of that is free; Pro is a $5 one-time purchase for every other website and extras. It runs locally, no account, no tracking, [source is public](https://github.com/qwemort/pure-spoiler-shield). It reads text, so a spoiler only inside a thumbnail image can slip through. [Get it for Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/aloilpojkoblacapdlknacjiljgjibng?utm_source=pureguides&utm_medium=demo). Disclosure: I build it.
