Cameos travel faster than plot

Big team-up films are spoiled by casting. "Who appears" and "who dies" spread as short captions and reaction thumbnails, often with no warning. Post-credits scenes are another favourite, because they hint at the next film.

Reports say Marvel plans advance screenings before December 18, and release dates differ by country, so expect leaks several days early. Muting every hero name hides half the internet, so mute the title with story words instead.

Questions

When does Avengers: Doomsday come out?

It is scheduled for cinemas on December 18, 2026. Some reports mention advance screenings a little earlier.

Which words should I mute?

The title with ending, leak, cameo, post-credits scene and who dies. Muting only "Avengers" also hides news you may want.

How long should I avoid spoilers?

The first two weeks after release are the riskiest, when reactions and memes are everywhere.

Can I mute words on YouTube?

No. YouTube has no keyword mute. In a desktop browser an extension can do it.
