How to avoid Avengers: Doomsday spoilers before you see it

Updated 17 November 2026

Avengers: Doomsday is scheduled to open in cinemas on December 18, 2026, the same day as Dune: Part Three. With a Marvel ensemble film the danger is not only the ending. It is who shows up: a single cameo in a thumbnail or a post title can be the surprise of the whole film.

Disclosure: I build a browser extension for this, Pure Spoiler Blocker. It is mentioned where it solves something the sites cannot. Everything else on this page works without installing anything.
Short version
  1. Mute the words below now, not on opening night.
  2. Expect leaks before December 18: reports say Marvel plans advance screenings ahead of the release date.
  3. Skip YouTube comments and Shorts about the film, and mute Marvel communities on Reddit.
  4. Do not search the title. Autocomplete finishes it with cameos and "post-credits".
  5. Plan for at least two weeks after release.

When the spoilers start

Reports from September say Marvel plans advance screenings before December 18 (source). Add premieres, press screenings and different release dates by country, and you get spoilers in feeds several days before you can buy a ticket. Set your filters this week.

Why this film is hard to protect

Big team-up films are spoiled by casting, not just by plot. "Who appears" and "who dies" travel as short captions and reaction thumbnails, often with no spoiler warning. Post-credits scenes are another favourite, because they hint at the next film. That is why muting only the title is not enough, and also why muting every hero name hides half of the internet.

Words to mute

No spoilers here, only the title plus general story words. Add both spellings people use.

Avengers Doomsday spoilers · Doomsday ending · Doomsday leak · Doomsday cameo · post-credits scene · Avengers 5 ending · who dies · ending explained

Settings: on X it is Settings and privacy, Privacy and safety, Mute and block, Muted words. On Threads and Instagram, Settings, Privacy, Hidden words. On TikTok, Settings and privacy, Content preferences, Filter keywords. The longer list with translations is on the Avengers: Doomsday mute list.

YouTube and Reddit have no word mute

YouTube only offers "Not interested" and "Don't recommend channel". Reddit mutes communities, not words. Thumbnails, titles and comments are where most spoilers land, and there is nothing to type a word into.

It is free and has a ready list for Avengers: Doomsday: switch it on and it blurs matching videos, Shorts, posts, comments and search suggestions on YouTube, Reddit and X, while leaving trailers and news alone. Add it to Chrome or Firefox. Text is checked in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere. It reads text only, so a spoiler that exists only inside an image gets through, and it works in desktop browsers, not in phone apps.

What no setting can catch

  • Images and video frames. A thumbnail can show the twist with no words at all.
  • Names nobody knows yet. Characters and actors who were kept secret before release cannot be listed in advance.
  • People. Friends and group chats. Tell them early that you have not seen it.

Questions

When does Avengers: Doomsday come out?

It is scheduled for cinemas on December 18, 2026. Some reports say Marvel plans advance screenings a little earlier.

Which words should I mute for Avengers: Doomsday?

The title with story words such as ending, leak, cameo, post-credits scene and who dies. Muting only "Avengers" also hides news and trailers you may want.

Can I mute words on YouTube?

No. YouTube has no keyword mute. To hide videos and comments by word in a desktop browser you need an extension.

How long should I avoid spoilers?

Until you have seen it. In practice the first two weeks after release are the riskiest, when reaction videos and memes are everywhere.
