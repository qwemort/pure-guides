Questions

When does Blade Runner 2099 come out?

All eight episodes arrive on Prime Video on November 25, 2026.

How long should I avoid spoilers?

Two or three weeks. Most people watch the whole season in the first days, so spoilers peak right away.

Which words should I mute?

The series name with words like finale, ending, spoilers and who dies. Muting only "Blade Runner" also hides the films.

Can I mute words on YouTube?

No. YouTube has no keyword mute. In a desktop browser an extension can blur matching videos and comments.
