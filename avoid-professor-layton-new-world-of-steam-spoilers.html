The ending is the whole point

In most games a spoiler ruins a scene. In Layton it ruins the reason to play: every chapter builds to who is behind it and why. Puzzle walkthroughs are safe to search, but videos about the final chapter put the answer in the title.

Copies can reach some players a few days before December 10. Do not mute plain "Layton": it also hides puzzle help and news about the series.

Where to paste them

XNotifications tab, gear icon, Muted, Muted words. Pick "Forever".
Threads, InstagramThreads: Profile, Menu, Content preferences, Hidden words, New filter. Instagram keeps its own list in Content preferences, Specific words and phrases (suggested posts only).
TikTokProfile, Menu, Settings and privacy, Content preferences, Filter keywords. Works in the For You and Following feeds, up to 100 words.
YouTube"Not interested" and "Don't recommend channel". Comments and Shorts stay unfiltered.
RedditMute communities from their menu. Titles in r/popular still get through.
DiscordMute busy servers and channels for the first weeks.

Questions

When does the new Professor Layton game come out?

On December 10, 2026, for Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, PS5 and PC.

Are puzzle solutions spoilers?

Usually not for the story. Search the puzzle number, not the chapter name, and avoid videos about the last chapters.

Which words should I mute?

The game title with words like ending, final chapter, culprit, true identity and spoilers. Muting only "Layton" also hides puzzle help.

Can I mute words on YouTube?

No. YouTube has no keyword mute. In a desktop browser an extension can blur matching videos and comments.
