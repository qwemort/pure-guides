The book is the biggest spoiler

For most films spoilers start at the premiere. For this one they already exist: plot summaries of Dune Messiah are one search away, and book readers bring them up under trailers, in "what to expect" videos and in casting news. If you have not read it, treat anything that mentions Messiah as a spoiler.

Film details then arrive a few days before December 18 from premieres and press screenings, and "book vs movie" breakdowns appear on opening night. Do not mute plain "Dune": it also hides Dune: Prophecy and unrelated news.

Questions

When does Dune: Part Three come out?

It is scheduled for cinemas on December 18, 2026.

Is it based on a book?

Yes, on Dune Messiah by Frank Herbert (1969), so the main story is widely discussed online.

Which words should I mute?

The film title and Dune Messiah, with words like ending, spoilers, who dies and book vs movie.

Can I mute words on YouTube?

No. YouTube has no keyword mute. In a desktop browser an extension can do it.
