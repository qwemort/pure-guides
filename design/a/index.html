How it works

Three steps. No account, no setup page to fight with.

01

Pick what to protect

52 ready lists for games, shows and anime. Add up to 3 of your own titles for free.

02

Scroll as usual

Videos, posts and comments that match your titles get blurred, with a short label saying why.

03

Two clicks to look

One click arms it, the second shows it. No accidental spoilers from a hover.

Saw a spoiler word the lists missed? Select it, right-click, choose “Hide as a spoiler”.
