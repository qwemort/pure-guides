GTA 6 blocked words for Twitch and Kick chat: copy-ready list for streamers

Updated 14 November 2026 · free to copy and share, no credit needed

GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and no PC version has been announced (source). If you play it blind on stream, the likeliest source of spoilers is your own chat. Below are words and phrases to put in your blocked terms before you go live. The list contains no spoilers: only the game's name, the two lead characters already shown in the trailers, and generic story words.

Tap a word to copy it, or copy a whole group (one word per line).

1. Generic spoiler words

Work for any game. The * at the end also blocks variations, for example "spoil*" blocks "spoiler" and "spoilers" on Twitch.

spoil*|ending*|leak*|final mission|last mission|plot twist|who dies|he dies|she dies

2. GTA 6 specific

Names from the official trailers. These are patterns, not hints about the story.

GTA 6 spoiler|GTA 6 spoilers|GTA VI spoiler|GTA VI spoilers|GTA 6 ending|GTA VI ending|GTA 6 final mission|GTA 6 leak|GTA 6 leaked|GTA 6 story|GTA 6 twist|Jason dies|Lucia dies|Jason death|Lucia death|Jason ending|Lucia ending|#GTA6Spoilers

3. Plain version, no wildcards

For a platform or chat bot that does not understand the *. It blocks fewer variations.

spoiler|spoilers|spoiled|ending|endings|leak|leaks|leaked

Where to paste them

  • Twitch: Creator Dashboard, Settings, Moderation, Blocked terms and phrases. Terms go in one at a time, and the dashboard has no bulk import that we know of. Twitch describes the * wildcard in its Creator Camp moderation guide.
  • Kick: Streamer Dashboard, chat moderation settings. Menu names change, so check the Kick Help Center if you do not see blocked words.
  • Chat bots: if your bot has its own banned words filter, paste the plain version there as well.

Before a blind stream

  1. Add the terms before you go live, not during the stream.
  2. Pin a chat rule that spoilers get a timeout, and tell your moderators which words are blocked.
  3. Spoilers also arrive as links and clips, so moderators should remove those by hand.
  4. Keep your own preparation spoiler-free too: this list protects your chat, not your browser.

A blocked word list cannot catch every spelling, and people can rephrase. Treat it as the first layer, not a guarantee.
