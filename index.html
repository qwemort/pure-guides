How it works

  1. Pick what to protect. 60 ready lists for games, shows and anime, plus as many titles of your own as you like.
  2. Scroll as usual. Videos, posts and comments that match get blurred, with a short label saying why.
  3. Two clicks to look. The first click arms it and turns the label yellow. The second shows it. No accidental spoilers from a hover.

Saw a spoiler word the lists missed? Select it, right-click, choose “Hide as a spoiler”.
