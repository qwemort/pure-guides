How to avoid GTA 6 spoilers on YouTube

YouTube has no word filter, so starve the recommendations and stay away from thumbnails, Shorts, comments and search suggestions. About five minutes:

  1. Clear and pause your watch history. History in the left menu, Clear all watch history, then Pause watch history. With history off and empty, the home page stops showing recommendations, and that is where most launch-week spoilers show up.
  2. Turn off autoplay. The switch is under the player. One autoplayed reaction video is enough to see the ending.
  3. Stay out of Shorts for the first weeks. A Short shows the frame before you read the title. If one slips through, use Don't recommend channel, not only Not interested.
  4. Do not type "GTA 6" into search. Suggestions fill in "ending explained" before you press Enter. Open the channel you want directly.
  5. Skip the comments under any video about the game, trailers and old news included. Top comments are where people spoil on purpose.
  6. Turn off the bell for channels that will stream or review the game, so notifications do not show a thumbnail.

If you play on PC

No PC release date has been announced. Plan for months, not days: use filters you can leave on, not a week offline. Set the core list once, keep YouTube history paused, and add names to the list only after the trailers you have seen.

Which words should I mute for GTA 6?

The game name in both spellings (GTA 6 and GTA VI) together with story words such as ending, leak, twist and who dies. Muting only the name also hides harmless news.
