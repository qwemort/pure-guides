How to avoid GTA 6 spoilers: words to mute, YouTube, Reddit

Updated 10 November 2026 · English · Українська · Polski

Grand Theft Auto VI comes out on November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC version has been announced. Almost every guide tells you to mute words on X, and that helps, but most spoilers arrive through YouTube thumbnails, Reddit titles and comments, where there is nothing to mute. This page covers both: the words, and what to do where words cannot be muted.

Disclosure: I build a browser extension for this, Pure Spoiler Blocker. It is mentioned where it solves something the sites cannot. Everything else on this page works without installing anything.
Short version
  1. Mute the words below on X, Threads and TikTok today, not on launch day.
  2. YouTube and Reddit cannot mute words. Use "Don't recommend channel", mute the game's communities, and stay out of comments, or let an extension blur them.
  3. Do not type the game's name into search boxes. Suggestions are a common leak.
  4. Mute busy group chats and Discord servers for the first weeks.
  5. Filters miss things. Images and video frames cannot be filtered by anyone.

Spoilers start before November 19

Do not wait for launch day. Review copies are already with the press (source), and big releases usually reach some players a few days early. Story details can show up in feeds before the game is officially out, so set your filters now.

Words to mute for GTA 6

These contain no spoilers, only the game's name and general story words. Add both spellings, GTA 6 and GTA VI.

GTA 6 ending · GTA VI ending · GTA 6 spoilers · GTA 6 leak · ending explained · final mission · secret ending · true ending · all endings · who dies · death scene · plot twist

The full copy-ready GTA 6 mute list has more words, plus Ukrainian, Polish and Russian. Muting just "GTA 6" also hides trailers and news, so keep that for launch week only.

X, Threads and TikTok: mute the words

  • X. Settings and privacy, Privacy and safety, Mute and block, Muted words. Choose "Forever" and apply it to the home timeline and notifications.
  • Threads and Instagram. Settings, Privacy, Hidden words. Add your own words and phrases to the custom list.
  • TikTok. Settings and privacy, Content preferences, Filter keywords. It works on captions and hashtags, so a clip with no text can still get through.

YouTube: there is no word filter

YouTube has no keyword mute. What it does give you:

  • In the three-dot menu on a video, Not interested and Don't recommend channel reduce similar videos.
  • Turn off autoplay, and do not scroll Shorts while the game is fresh. A Short can show the ending before you see its title.
  • Skip the comments on any video about the game, including trailers.
  • Remove GTA videos from your watch history, so the recommendations calm down.

Reddit: mute the communities

Reddit mutes communities, not words. Mute the main GTA communities from their menu so they leave your home feed. r/popular and search can still show the game, and a post title alone can be the spoiler. Spoiler tags are optional and many people skip them.

Google and other search boxes

  • Do not search the game's name. Autocomplete can finish it with "ending" or a character name.
  • If you need help with a mission, search the practical thing, not the game plus a story word.

Discord, group chats and people

Many spoilers come from friends. Say it once, early: "I have not finished it, please use a spoiler warning." Mute busy Discord servers and group chats for the first weeks, and unfollow streamers who will play the story on day one.

What no setting can catch

  • Words you did not think of. Names that appear only in the finished game cannot be listed in advance.
  • Images and video frames. A thumbnail can spoil something no text filter will ever see.
  • Phone apps. Extensions work in desktop browsers. In the apps you only have the built-in settings above.

Questions

Can you mute words on YouTube?

No. YouTube has no keyword mute. "Not interested" and "Don't recommend channel" only reduce similar videos. To hide videos and comments by word in a desktop browser you need an extension.

When do GTA 6 spoilers start?

Before launch day. Review copies are already with the press and big games usually reach some players a few days early, so story details can appear before November 19, 2026.

Which words should I mute for GTA 6?

The game name in both spellings (GTA 6 and GTA VI) together with story words such as ending, final mission, secret ending, who dies and leak. Muting only the name also hides harmless news.

Is there a GTA 6 spoiler blocker extension?

Yes, several. Pure Spoiler Blocker is a free one for Chrome and Firefox that blurs spoiler videos, posts and comments on YouTube, Reddit and X and checks the text locally.

How long do I need to avoid spoilers if I play on PC?

There is no PC release date yet, so plan for months, not days. Use filters you can leave on rather than staying offline.
