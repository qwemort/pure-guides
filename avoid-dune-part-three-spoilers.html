How to avoid Dune: Part Three spoilers (even if the book is old)

Updated 17 November 2026

Dune: Part Three opens in cinemas on December 18, 2026, the same day as Avengers: Doomsday. It is based on Dune Messiah, the second Dune novel, published in 1969. That changes the problem: the main story has been public for decades, and book readers discuss it openly, often without any warning.

Disclosure: I build a browser extension for this, Pure Spoiler Blocker. It is mentioned where it solves something the sites cannot. Everything else on this page works without installing anything.
Short version
  1. Mute "Dune Messiah" as well as the film title. Book discussions spoil the film.
  2. Mute the words below now, not on opening night.
  3. Stay out of "book vs movie" videos and threads until you have seen it.
  4. Do not search the title. Autocomplete suggests ending and character questions.
  5. Do not mute plain "Dune": it hides Dune: Prophecy and unrelated news too.

The book is the biggest spoiler

For most films, spoilers start at the premiere. For this one they already exist: plot summaries of Dune Messiah are a search away, and fans of the books bring them up in comments under trailers, in "what to expect" videos and in casting news. If you have not read the book, treat anything that mentions Messiah as a spoiler.

When the film spoilers start

Premieres, press screenings and different release dates by country mean details from the film itself reach feeds a few days before December 18. "Book vs movie" breakdowns appear on opening night and are full of both.

Words to mute

No spoilers here, only titles plus general story words. The book title matters as much as the film title.

Dune Part Three spoilers · Dune 3 ending · Dune Messiah ending · Dune Messiah spoilers · book vs movie · ending explained · who dies · Dune leak

Settings: on X it is Settings and privacy, Privacy and safety, Mute and block, Muted words. On Threads and Instagram, Settings, Privacy, Hidden words. On TikTok, Settings and privacy, Content preferences, Filter keywords. The longer list with translations is on the Dune: Part Three mute list.

YouTube and Reddit have no word mute

YouTube only offers "Not interested" and "Don't recommend channel". Reddit mutes communities, not words. Thumbnails, titles and comments are where most spoilers land, and there is nothing to type a word into.

Add it to Chrome or Firefox. Text is checked in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere. It reads text only, so a spoiler that exists only inside an image gets through, and it works in desktop browsers, not in phone apps.

What no setting can catch

  • Images and video frames. A thumbnail can show the twist with no words at all.
  • Names nobody knows yet. Changes the film makes to the book cannot be predicted, so no list catches them in advance.
  • People. Friends and group chats. Tell them early that you have not seen it.

Questions

When does Dune: Part Three come out?

It is scheduled for cinemas on December 18, 2026.

Is Dune: Part Three based on a book?

Yes. It adapts Dune Messiah by Frank Herbert, published in 1969, so the main story is already widely discussed online.

Which words should I mute for Dune: Part Three?

The film title and Dune Messiah, together with words such as ending, spoilers, who dies and book vs movie. Muting only "Dune" also hides Dune: Prophecy and general news.

Can I mute words on YouTube?

No. YouTube has no keyword mute. To hide videos and comments by word in a desktop browser you need an extension.
