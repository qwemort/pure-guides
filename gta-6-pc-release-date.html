GTA 6 PC release date: what is known

Updated 4 November 2026. I update this page when there is official news.

Short version
  • GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S (source).
  • No PC version has been announced, and there is no official PC date.
  • Rockstar's last two big games reached PC about 1 to 1.5 years after consoles. That is history, not a promise.

What Take-Two has said

In a Bloomberg interview published on May 5, 2026, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick explained the console-first launch: "Rockstar always starts on console because I think with regard to a release like that you're judged by serving the core." He did not confirm a PC version or give a date (source).
