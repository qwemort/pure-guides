Everyone knows the book, not the show

The story is from 1997, so spoilers here are different: new scenes, changed endings of episodes, cut characters and casting surprises. Those show up in reaction videos, memes and "book vs show" threads within hours of each episode.

Leaks from screenings can appear a few days before December 25. Do not mute plain "Harry Potter": it also hides the films, games and everything else in the franchise.

Where to paste them

XNotifications tab, gear icon, Muted, Muted words. Pick "Forever".
Threads, InstagramThreads: Profile, Menu, Content preferences, Hidden words, New filter. Instagram keeps its own list in Content preferences, Specific words and phrases (suggested posts only).
TikTokProfile, Menu, Settings and privacy, Content preferences, Filter keywords. Works in the For You and Following feeds, up to 100 words.
YouTube"Not interested" and "Don't recommend channel". Comments and Shorts stay unfiltered.
RedditMute communities from their menu. Titles in r/popular still get through.
DiscordMute busy servers and channels for the first weeks.

Questions

When does the Harry Potter series start?

The first season premieres on HBO and HBO Max on December 25, 2026.

What can be spoiled if I read the book?

Changes: new scenes, cut characters, a different order of events and casting reveals. Those are discussed right after each episode.

Which words should I mute?

The series name with words like episode, ending, spoilers and book vs show. Muting only "Harry Potter" also hides the films.

Can I mute words on YouTube?

No. YouTube has no keyword mute. In a desktop browser an extension can blur matching videos and comments.
